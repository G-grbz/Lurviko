Fluent icon theme
https://github.com/vinceliuice/Fluent-icon-theme

Lurviko's interface glyphs are taken from the Fluent icon theme by
Vince Liuice <vinceliuice@hotmail.com>. Some icons were renamed, resized or
edited (for example to normalize their view box) for use in Lurviko, and are
recolored at runtime.

According to its AUTHORS file, the Fluent icon theme itself draws on:
  - numix-icon-theme-circle  https://github.com/numixproject/numix-icon-theme-circle
  - papirus-icon-theme       https://github.com/PapirusDevelopmentTeam/papirus-icon-theme
  - paper-icon-theme         https://github.com/snwh/paper-icon-theme
